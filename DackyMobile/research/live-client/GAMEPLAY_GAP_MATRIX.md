1. **PvE/Bất Chu Sơn + combat trace**: party/commander, route rewards, waves, carry, loss/withdrawal, report/replay, battle event schema.
2. **Permanent hero growth + buffs**: Ma Trận theo từng tướng, title expiry, item buff duration/stacking, alliance passive.
3. **Scheduled events**: eligibility/timezone, active/drop tables, claim/exchange, attendance/online reward.
4. **Competitive activities**: tower/strongpoint signup, bracket/multi-guild match, shop/points; tách PvP khỏi PvE.
5. **City and tech**: building predicates, per-city resources/tech effects, guide CTA predicates, outside-city harvest.
